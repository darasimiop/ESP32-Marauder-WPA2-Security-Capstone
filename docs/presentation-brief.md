# Capstone presentation brief

1. **Scope.** I built and validated an ESP32 wireless security prototype in an instructor-authorized lab.
2. **Troubleshooting.** The device booted, but a functional test showed a microSD mount failure. My final report records a successful mount after solder rework.
3. **Evidence.** Early packet captures were incomplete. I documented how evidence quality affected the conclusions I could draw, and what a later complete exchange established.
4. **Finding.** An initial offline assessment produced no match. A later assessment recovered the lab passphrase, illustrating the risk of guessable WPA2-Personal credentials.
5. **Takeaway.** Validate each stage, record both failures and successes, and report conclusions within the scope of the test.
